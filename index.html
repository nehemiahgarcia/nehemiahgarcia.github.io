How a clip gets made

long-form → short-form
  1. Watch it allI go through the full podcast, webinar or interview myself instead of relying only on pre-picked timestamps.
  2. Find the hookI pull moments that make sense on their own and put the strongest line in the first few seconds.
  3. Trim for retentionI cut dead air and tangents, and tighten the pacing until there's no reason to scroll away.
  4. Caption & polishI add captions, hook titles, graphics, color and sound, in English or Spanish.
  5. Format & reviseI export per platform, then apply the team's feedback to the next batch of clips.
